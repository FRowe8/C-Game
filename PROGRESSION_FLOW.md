# Game Progression & Narrative Flow

## Game Concept: Quantum Spaceship Commander

You're the commander of a lone research vessel exploring quantum space. As you journey deeper into the cosmos, you'll unlock new systems, recruit crew through summons, upgrade your ship, and battle hostile forces in different galactic zones.

---

## Progression Tiers

### 🎯 Tier 0: Tutorial & Core Loop (Levels 1-5)
**Unlocked from Start:**
- **Idle Resource Generation** (Qubits/Coherence/Entanglement)
- **Research Tree** - Basic upgrades
- **Stats Screen** - Track your progress

**Narrative:**
> "Your research vessel has just left the space station. Focus on gathering quantum resources and researching basic ship systems."

**Goal:** Learn the idle game loop - resources generate passively, research unlocks upgrades

**UI Focus:**
- Main screen shows resources clearly
- Research tree is prominent
- Tutorial tooltips explain clicking and idle mechanics

---

### 🚀 Tier 1: Ship Customization (Level 5)
**Unlocks at Level 5:**
- **Spaceship Screen** - View and customize your ship
- **Achievements** - Track milestones

**Narrative:**
> "Commander, we've scanned our vessel's systems. You can now access the ship customization interface."

**Goal:** Understand ship stats and how parts affect performance

**Tutorial:**
1. "This is your ship. It has slots for different parts."
2. "Parts provide bonuses to Power, Defense, Speed, and Production."
3. "Better parts will be available as you progress."

---

### ⚔️ Tier 2: Combat Introduction (Level 10)
**Unlocks at Level 10:**
- **Combat System** - Turn-based battles
- **Zone 1: Asteroid Belt** (Levels 1-20, 15 stages)
- **Milestones** - Long-term goals

**Narrative:**
> "ALERT! Hostile contacts detected in the Asteroid Belt. Engage combat protocols!"

**Goal:** Learn combat mechanics and zone progression

**Tutorial:**
1. "Enemies lurk in different zones. Click COMBAT to engage."
2. "Combat is turn-based. Use Attack, Defend, or Special abilities."
3. "Defeating enemies grants Credits, XP, and may drop ship parts!"
4. "Complete all 15 stages to unlock the next zone."

**Combat Introduction Battle:**
- First combat is against a Level 1 Scout (easy)
- Guaranteed to drop a Common part
- Tutorial highlights combat buttons

---

### 🎰 Tier 3: Summon System (Level 15)
**Unlocks at Level 15:**
- **Summon/Gatcha System** - Pull for ship parts
- **Buyables** - Purchase permanent upgrades

**Narrative:**
> "Commander! We've established contact with the Galactic Parts Exchange. You can now summon rare ship components!"

**Goal:** Understand the gatcha system and rarity tiers

**Tutorial:**
1. "Summons use Credits to pull random ship parts."
2. "Three banners available: Basic, Advanced, Elite."
3. "Rarity tiers: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary"
4. "Pity system guarantees rare drops after X pulls."

**First Summon Bonus:**
- Player gets 1 free 10-pull on first access
- Guaranteed Rare or better in first 10-pull

---

### 🌟 Tier 4: Skill Tree (Level 20)
**Unlocks at Level 20:**
- **Skill Tree** - Permanent character progression
- **Zone 2: Nebula Frontier** (Levels 20-40)

**Narrative:**
> "Your crew has analyzed combat data. New skill training protocols unlocked!"

**Goal:** Learn skill system and branching choices

**Tutorial:**
1. "Skills are permanent upgrades across all playthroughs."
2. "Three branches: Combat, Engineering, Exploration."
3. "Earn 1 Skill Point per level."
4. "Skills require prerequisites - plan your build!"

**Skill Point Grant:**
- Player receives retroactive skill points for levels 1-20
- Can immediately spend 20 points
- Guides them through first skill choice

---

### ⚡ Tier 5: Enhancement System (Level 30)
**Unlocks at Level 30:**
- **Enhancement System** - Upgrade existing parts
- **Zone 3: Derelict Fleet** (Levels 40-60)
- **Challenges** - Special difficulty modes

**Narrative:**
> "Engineering bay operational! We can now enhance ship parts beyond their base specifications."

**Goal:** Learn enhancement and star ascension

**Tutorial:**
1. "Enhancement increases part stats by +10% per level (max +15)."
2. "Success rate decreases at higher levels - be strategic!"
3. "Star Ascension grants +25% per star (max 6 stars)."
4. "Dismantle unwanted parts for enhancement materials."

**Enhancement Materials:**
- Drop from combat (level-scaled)
- Dismantle duplicate parts
- Four material tiers unlock progressively

---

### 🌌 Tier 6: Advanced Zones (Levels 40-80)
**Unlocks Progressively:**
- **Level 40:** Zone 3 - Derelict Fleet
- **Level 60:** Zone 4 - Black Hole Horizon
- **Level 80:** Zone 5 - Ancient Armada

**Narrative:**
> "Long-range scanners detecting new hostile zones. Extreme danger - high rewards."

**Zone Unlock Requirements:**
- Minimum level
- Complete previous zone 100%
- Pay unlock cost (Credits)

**Zone Difficulty Scaling:**
1. Asteroid Belt: Training grounds
2. Nebula Frontier: First real challenge
3. Derelict Fleet: Elite enemies
4. Black Hole Horizon: Void entities
5. Ancient Armada: Legendary bosses

---

### 💎 Tier 7: Prestige Systems (Level 50+)
**Unlocks at Level 50:**
- **Essence Shop** - Spend Essence for permanent bonuses
- **Singularity Shop** - Powerful prestige upgrades

**Narrative:**
> "Quantum anomalies detected! Harvesting Essence can grant permanent power increases."

**Prestige Mechanics:**
1. Reset progress for powerful permanent bonuses
2. Keep: Skills, Achievements, Prestige Currency
3. Reset: Levels, Resources, Ship Parts
4. Gain: Essence (soft prestige), Singularities (hard prestige)

```
+----------------------------------+
| Resources: Q, C, E               |

+----------------------------------+
| [RESEARCH] [COMBAT] [SUMMON]     |
| [SKILLS]   [ENHANCE] [MORE...]   |

+----------------------------------+

```

├── RESEARCH (Always available)

## Onboarding Flow

### First Launch
1. **Welcome Screen:** "Welcome, Commander!"
2. **Tutorial Tooltip:** "Click to generate Qubits"
3. **First Research:** Guide player to research tree
4. **First Upgrade:** Unlock first research node
5. **Idle Explanation:** "Resources generate even when you're away!"

### Level 5 Unlock
- **Popup:** "NEW FEATURE: Spaceship Customization"
- **Tutorial:** Briefly show spaceship screen
- **Hint:** "Better parts will help in future combat"

### Level 10 Unlock (Combat)
- **Dramatic Popup:** "ALERT! Combat Systems Online"
- **Forced Tutorial Battle:** Easy fight with tooltips
- **Reward:** First guaranteed part drop
- **Unlock:** Zone 1 available

### Level 15 Unlock (Summon)
- **Popup:** "Galactic Parts Exchange Available"
- **Free Gift:** 1 free 10-pull
- **Tutorial:** Show banner types and rarity system
- **Excitement:** Guaranteed Rare+ in first pull

### Level 20 Unlock (Skills)
- **Popup:** "Skill Training Available"
- **Skill Point Grant:** Receive 20 retroactive points
- **Tutorial:** Explain three branches
- **Choice:** Let player choose first skill

### Level 30 Unlock (Enhancement)
- **Popup:** "Engineering Bay Operational"
- **Tutorial:** Enhance a part for free (100% success)
- **Materials:** Grant starter materials
- **Goal:** Show path to 6-star Legendary parts

## Progression Goals Per Tier

### Early Game (Lv 1-20): Learning
- **Goal:** Understand core systems
- **Achievement:** "Complete first zone"
- **Milestone:** Reach level 20

### Mid Game (Lv 20-50): Optimization
- **Goal:** Build optimal ship and skill loadout
- **Achievement:** "Enhance a part to +15"
- **Milestone:** Complete 3 zones

### Late Game (Lv 50-80): Mastery
- **Goal:** Tackle endgame zones and challenges
- **Achievement:** "Obtain a 6-star Legendary part"
- **Milestone:** Defeat Black Hole Horizon boss

### Endgame (Lv 80+): Prestige Preparation
- **Goal:** Complete Ancient Armada, prepare for prestige
- **Achievement:** "Defeat the Ancient Dreadnought"
- **Milestone:** Unlock all skills

## Feature Synergies

### Combat ↔ Summon ↔ Enhancement Loop
1. **Combat** → Earn Credits & Materials
2. **Summon** → Get new parts with Credits
3. **Enhancement** → Upgrade parts with Materials
4. **Stronger Ship** → Tackle harder Combat
5. **Repeat** (virtuous cycle)

### Skills ↔ Combat Synergy
- Combat skills boost damage/defense
- Engineering skills boost production
- Exploration skills boost drop rates
- **All skills persist through prestige**

### Zones ↔ Progression Gate
- Each zone has level requirement
- Must complete previous zone
- Unlock cost scales with zone tier
- **Creates natural progression curve**

## Mobile-First Considerations

### Touch Targets
- All buttons: Minimum 44px × 44px
- Zone selection: Large touch zones
- Part selection: Easy to tap on small screens

### Screen Layout
- Portrait mode optimized
- Important info in top 60% (thumb reach)
- Buttons in bottom 40% (easy tapping)

### Information Hierarchy
- Most critical: Resources, Level, Current Goal
- Secondary: Available features (unlocked)
- Tertiary: Locked features (grayed out)
