## 🎓 Lessons Learned

### What Worked
- Quantum theme is engaging
- Particle effects feel satisfying
- Clear progression with milestones
- Offline progress hooks players back
- Web build removes barriers

### What Didn't Work
- Initial MinGW complexity (fixed)
- Too many systems at once (streamlined)
- Numbers getting too big (formatting fixed)

### Future Considerations
- Start with simpler build system
- Add content gradually
- Listen to player feedback early
- Balance is ongoing process
