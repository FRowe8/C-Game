# Quantum Idle - Marketing & Features

## 🎯 Unique Selling Points

### "The Most Scientific Idle Game Ever Made"

Quantum Idle isn't just another idle game - it's a journey through the quantum realm where you'll discover the mysteries of the universe while building an empire of quantum research stations.

## 🌟 Marquee Features

### 1. **Quantum Superposition System** ⚛️
**UNIQUE**: Resources accumulate in quantum superposition - uncertain states that you "collapse" to gain resources!

- **Visual Wave Function**: Watch resources build up in a probabilistic state
- **Observation Mechanic**: Click to collapse the wave function
- **Risk vs Reward**: High probability = stable gains, low probability = big jackpots
- **Strategic Timing**: Observe at the right moment for maximum rewards

**Marketing Angle**: *"Your choices matter - every observation is a gamble!"*

### 2. **Research Discovery Tree** 🔬
**ROBUST**: 20+ quantum physics discoveries to unlock, from basic mechanics to exotic phenomena

**Tiers**:
- **Tier 1**: Quantum Mechanics 101, Wave Function Theory
- **Tier 2**: Quantum Computing, Superposition Mastery
- **Tier 3**: Quantum Field Theory, String Theory
- **Tier 4**: Multiverse Theory, Quantum Singularity

**Each research unlocks**:
- New game mechanics
- Powerful production bonuses
- Automation features
- Visual effects

**Marketing Angle**: *"Unlock the secrets of the universe, one discovery at a time!"*

### 3. **Particle Collection System** 🔬
**ENGAGING**: Collect 28 exotic particles from Electrons to God Particles!

**Rarity Tiers**:
- Common: Electrons, Protons, Neutrons
- Uncommon: Muons, Positrons
- Rare: 6 Types of Quarks
- Exotic: Higgs Boson, W/Z Bosons
- Legendary: Graviton, Tachyon, Monopole
- Mythical: Quantum Singularity, Dark Matter, Dark Energy

**Gameplay**:
- Random discoveries while playing
- Each particle grants unique bonuses
- Equip up to 3 particles at once
- Collection completion rewards

**Marketing Angle**: *"Gotta catch 'em all - but with REAL particle physics!"*

### 4. **Quantum Experiments** 🧪
**EXCITING**: 8 unique challenge modes with exclusive rewards!

**Experiments Include**:
- **Double-Slit Experiment**: Observation changes reality
- **Schrödinger's Box**: Quantum superposition test
- **Quantum Teleportation**: Instant resource transfer
- **Wave Collapse Race**: Speed challenge
- **Time Reversal**: Run physics backwards

**Each experiment**:
- Tests different skills
- Time-limited challenges
- Unique particle rewards
- Unlocks at different prestige levels

**Marketing Angle**: *"Prove you're a quantum physicist with real experiments!"*

### 5. **Milestone System** 🏆
**CLEAR PROGRESSION**: 20+ major milestones with HUGE rewards

**Categories**:
- **Wealth Milestones**: First Thousand → First Trillion qubits
- **Station Milestones**: 5 Stations → All Maxed Out
- **Prestige Milestones**: First Prestige → 100 Total Photons
- **Time Milestones**: 1 Hour → 1 Week Played
- **Completion Milestones**: Half Achievements → True Ending

**Each milestone grants**:
- Massive resource rewards
- **Permanent production bonuses**
- Feature unlocks
- Bragging rights

**Marketing Angle**: *"Every milestone feels like a major accomplishment!"*

### 6. **Random Quantum Events** ⚡
**FOMO**: 7 powerful events that can trigger at any moment!

Events:
- Wave Collapse Bonus (2x observation rewards)
- Coherence Surge (infinite stability)
- Quantum Fluctuation (instant resources)
- Time Dilation (2x production)
- Resource Rain (500 qubits from the sky)

**Marketing Angle**: *"Keep the game open - you never know when an event will strike!"*

### 7. **Offline Progress** 💤
**RETENTION**: Earn resources for up to 4 hours while away!

- 50% efficiency (fair and balanced)
- Displays exactly what you earned
- Special "Offline Millionaire" achievement
- Daily login streak rewards

**Marketing Angle**: *"Play your way - even sleep rewards you!"*

### 8. **14 Achievements + Statistics** 📊
**ENGAGEMENT**: Track everything!

**Achievements include**:
- First Steps, Observer, Millionaire
- Speed Runner (prestige under 10 min)
- Hoarder (save 100M qubits)
- Quantum Master (prestige level 10)
- Week Streak (play 7 days straight)

**Statistics track**:
- Lifetime totals
- Session stats

- Daily streaks

**Marketing Angle**: *"Your quantum journey, quantified!"*

### 9. **Prestige System** 🔄
**DEPTH**: "Quantum Leap" - Reset for permanent bonuses!

- Earn Photons (prestige currency)
- Each photon = +10% ALL production (forever!)
- Unlock prestige-only research
- Faster progression each reset

**Marketing Angle**: *"Every reset makes you stronger!"*

### 10. **Visual Particle Effects** ✨
**POLISH**: Every action has satisfying visual feedback!

- Particles burst when gaining resources
- Achievement unlock animations

- Professional UI transitions

**Marketing Angle**: *"Feels as good as it looks!"*
